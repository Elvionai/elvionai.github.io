Meet the hands behind the look

Seyifunmi, known as Sunmmy Diva

Sunmmy Luxe is a Lagos beauty and style brand for people who want every part of their look to belong together. Makeup, hand-beaded outfits, and carefully chosen accessories come from one hand, so the whole look agrees with itself.

It began with needle and thread. Seyifunmi spent long hours beading fabric until every single bead sat right. Then she learned makeup, because she wanted more than to dress people. She wanted them to look in the mirror and smile.

That smile is why I do this work.

Every look is meant to leave you feeling confident, bold, and completely yourself.

Questions, answered

Do I have to pay a deposit?

Yes, for makeup bookings. A 70% deposit confirms your date. The balance is due once the service is done.

Can my makeup match my outfit?

Yes. Send a photo of your outfit and we will plan the look around it.

Will my makeup last?

Our glam is made to hold through photos, content shoots, and dancing.

How much are your accessories?

Prices depend on the piece. Send a screenshot or the name of what you like and we will reply with the price.

When are accessory orders delivered?

Accessory orders are delivered on weekends. Share your date when you message, and we will confirm if it works.

Do you make custom beading outfits for men?

Yes, including custom senator wear. Message us with what you have in mind.

What if I am not happy with my item?

Our promise on products is simple. If your item does not meet your standard, we will refund you and take it back.
