    Choose your register in the first sentence and keep it to the last.

    Evaluating
    • This is convincing because...
    • I am not convinced that...
    • However, this overlooks...
    • Although the text suggests..., ...
    • On balance, ...
    Persuading (mainly formal)
    • I would respectfully ask you to...
    • Please consider that...
    • It is clear that...
    • For these reasons, I urge you to...
    • I would be grateful if you would...
    • It would be in the interest of...
    Own words, not lifting
    • Read the idea, cover it, write it from memory in a new structure.
    • Change the word type (confident to confidence).
    • Use accurate synonyms and change the sentence order.
    Sentence variety

    Open some sentences with a prepositional phrase, then add a comma.

    • in my opinion,
    • despite the risks,
    • during exam season,
    • for many students,
    Five-paragraph plan
    • Opening: greet, say why you are writing, show your view (30 to 40 words)
    • Paragraphs 2 and 3: first bullet (120 to 140)
    • Paragraph 4: second bullet (80 to 90)
    • Closing: clear recommendation or request, then sign-off (30 to 40)
