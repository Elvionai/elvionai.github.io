    Privacy Policy

    At Elvion AI, privacy is a foundational engineering standard. Learn exactly what data we collect, how your prompts and multimodal uploads are protected across Philadelphia AI, and how to exercise your data rights.

    Terms & Conditions Privacy Desk

    Private Workspace

    Your conversations, uploaded files, and studio creations are private. We do not sell your personal data or train public models on your chats.

    Zero Card Storage

    Payments are processed via Korapay. Elvion AI never collects, views, or stores your raw credit card numbers or bank login credentials.

    Memory Toggle Control

    You control Cross-Chat Memory with a single toggle in Settings and can delete individual conversations or Gallery items immediately.

    30-Day Full Erasure

    Export your chats anytime as Markdown or request complete account and data deletion via hello@elvionai.com.ng.

    Section 01

    1. Information We Collect

    To provide our conversational, multimodal vision, and creative studio services across Elvion AI and Philadelphia AI, we collect only the minimum information necessary:

    • Account & Identity Information: When you register or sign in via Elvion AI, we collect your display name, email address, unique Firebase authentication UID, and optional profile avatar URL.
    • Financial & Wallet Transaction Data: When you fund your Pay As You Go credit wallet, payment processing is handled directly by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor, Korapay. We record your transaction reference, NGN/USD amount, and credit allocation, but Elvion AI never collects, processes, or stores your raw credit/debit card numbers or bank account passwords.
    • Workspace Inputs & Generated Outputs: We process the text prompts, code snippets, documents, PDFs, images, audio files, videos, and URLs you submit ("Inputs") and the responses or media generated for you ("Outputs").
    • Technical & API Telemetry: We collect standard diagnostic logs including IP address, browser/device type, timestamp, credit deduction logs, and Developer API endpoint usage to prevent fraud and maintain service reliability.
    Section 02

    2. How We Use Your Information and Legal Bases

    We process your information strictly to operate, secure, and improve your Elvion AI experience:

    • Service Delivery (Contractual Necessity): Authenticating your session, streaming Philadelphia AI responses, executing studio tools, syncing your chat history and Gallery across your devices, and deploying websites you create.
    • Wallet & Credit Accounting: Deducting credits for completed runs, issuing automatic credit refunds if a tool run fails on the server, and managing daily free credit claims and referral rewards.
    • Platform Security & Abuse Prevention (Legitimate Interest): Protecting our APIs and studios against automated attacks, unauthorized key sharing, payment fraud, and violations of our Acceptable Use Policy.
    • Optional Notifications (Consent): Sending browser or device pop-up notifications when a background task or reply finishes, only when you explicitly grant notification permission.
    Section 03

    3. Private Conversations and Zero Model Training

    Our Core Privacy Promise: Elvion AI does not sell your data, does not read your private conversations, and does not use your private workspace prompts, uploads, or outputs to train foundational AI models.

    • Automated Processing Only: Your conversations with Philadelphia AI are processed automatically by our inference and orchestration servers. Human staff do not review your private chats unless you explicitly submit a conversation to support for debugging or unless required by law to investigate a severe safety violation.
    • Developer API Confidentiality: Payloads sent through `/api/developer/*` using an Elvion Developer API key are strictly confidential and isolated to your account.
    Section 04

    4. Multimodal File Uploads, Video Vision, and Live Voice Calls

    • Document, Image, Audio & Video Uploads: When you attach files in Philadelphia Studio, they are used solely to answer your questions, extract context, or perform the edits you request. Temporary cloud artifacts are cached for approximately 10 days so you can view and download them in your chat and Gallery, after which expired cloud artifacts are purged.
    • Live Voice Calls & Microphone Access: When you initiate a Live Voice Call or use the in-browser Voice Studio, your browser requests microphone permission. Live call audio is streamed over encrypted WebSockets solely to generate real-time spoken responses and live captions, and is not permanently recorded or sold.
    Section 05

    5. Cross-Chat Memory and Local Device Storage

    • Cross-Chat Memory Toggle: Philadelphia Studio features a "Remember across chats" toggle in Settings. When enabled, your workspace automatically references recent messages from your own other conversations in Firestore so Philadelphia remembers context across threads. You can turn this toggle off at any time in Settings.
    • Local IndexedDB & Draft Persistence: To ensure instant 0ms file attachment and prevent lost work when you refresh a page or lose internet connection, your browser stores active composer drafts, attached file previews, and cached Gallery items locally on your device using `localStorage` and `IndexedDB`. Clearing your browser data or signing out removes local session caches.
    Section 06

    6. Infrastructure Partners and Sub-Processors

    To deliver low-latency authentication, payments, and cloud deployment, we work with enterprise infrastructure providers bound by strict data-processing and confidentiality obligations:

    • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore): Manages encrypted user authentication, session tokens, chat synchronization, and credit ledger records.
    • Korapay: Processes NGN/USD wallet top-up transactions securely.
    • Cloud Compute & Web Deployment Providers: Host our FastAPI backend and deploy static HTML5 websites when you explicitly click "Publish" or "Create Website" in the Studio.
    Section 07

    7. Cookies and Google AdSense (Public Blog)

    • Essential Workspace Storage: Inside the Studio, we use essential authentication tokens and local preferences (such as your active theme, selected persona, and voice settings) to keep your workspace functioning smoothly.
    • Google AdSense on Public Pages: Certain public informational pages, such as the Elvion Blog, may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
    • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
    Section 08

    8. Data Security and Encryption

    We implement defence-in-depth technical safeguards to protect your information:

    • All traffic between your browser/application and Elvion AI servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS and secure WebSockets (`WSS`).
    • Workspace requests require verified Firebase ID tokens (`X-Firebase-Token`) or authenticated Developer API bearer keys, enforcing strict per-user data isolation.
    • SSRF (Server-Side Request Forgery) protections block private-network targeting on URL-reading endpoints.
    Section 09

    9. Data Retention, Export, and Anytime Deletion

    • Self-Service Deletion: You can delete individual chat conversations and Gallery creations directly inside the Studio at any time with immediate effect.
    • Self-Service Export: You can export any conversation as a Markdown (`.md`) file from the Settings panel and download any generated image, video, audio file, code file, or PDF directly to your device.
    • Full Account Erasure (Within 30 Days): You may request complete deletion of your Elvion AI account and all associated personal data by emailing hello@elvionai.com.ng from your registered email address. We complete verified erasure requests within 30 days, retaining only anonymized financial ledger entries where strictly required by tax or financial law.
    Section 10

    10. Children's Privacy (13+ Policy)

    Elvion AI is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Users aged 13 to 17 may use the platform only with parent or legal guardian consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to Elvion AI, please contact us at hello@elvionai.com.ng and we will promptly delete the account and associated data.

    Section 11

    11. Your Global Privacy Rights (NDPA, GDPR, CCPA)

    Regardless of where you live, we extend strong data protection rights to all Elvion AI users, aligned with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

    • Right to Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data associated with your account or export your chats directly in-app.
    • Right to Rectification: Update your display name, avatar, and preferences directly in your Workspace panel at any time.
    • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Delete individual chats in-app or request full account deletion.
    • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Toggle off Cross-Chat Memory, disable notifications, or revoke API keys at any time.
