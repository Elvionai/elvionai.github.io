Meet Seyifunmi

Sunmmy Luxe began with needle and thread. I spent long hours beading fabric, until every single bead sat right. Then I learned makeup — because I wanted more than to dress people. I wanted them to look in the mirror and smile.

That smile is why I do this work. Every look is meant to leave you feeling confident, bold, and completely yourself. Makeup, beading, and accessories — all designed under one roof, all from one vision.

— Sunmmy Diva
